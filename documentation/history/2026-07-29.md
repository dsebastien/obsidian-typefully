# 2026-07-29 — Excluded tags

Added an `excludedTags` setting (issue #8) complementing `appendTags`: organizational tags such as `permanent_notes` or `literature_notes` can be listed once in the settings and are then filtered out before tags are appended to a post. Stored as `string[]` (without the leading `#`), edited through a text area that accepts comma/newline separated entries (`parse-excluded-tags.fn.ts` handles parsing and formatting, deduplicating case-insensitively while preserving the casing the user typed).

Matching lives in `filter-excluded-tags.fn.ts` and is deliberately forgiving: the leading `#` is optional on both sides, comparison is case-insensitive, and an excluded tag also excludes its nested children (`dev` excludes `dev/frontend`) since Obsidian users organize hierarchically — but only on a `/` boundary, so `dev` does not exclude `development`. Existing configs without the key take the empty default and trigger a settings re-save through the usual `needToSaveSettings` path.
